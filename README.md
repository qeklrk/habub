# 💪 Swole to the Stars

An idle/clicker game: lift weights in your mom's garage until you become **intergalactic**.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no build.

- **Lift** (click, tap, or hold Space) to earn strength and money. Fast lifting builds a combo multiplier.
- **Shop**: 20 upgrades, from heavier plates and protein shakes to clone labs, alien coaches and Dyson Sphere gyms.
- **Travel** through 11 places: Garage → Gym → Pro Competition → Everest → Orbit → Moon → Mars → Jupiter → the Sun → Andromeda (INTERGALACTIC) → Edge of the Universe.
- **Meteors** ⭐ fly by now and then. Click them for cash, strength, or a 7x frenzy.
- **Big Bang rebirth**: once intergalactic, reset for permanent Stardust boosts and cosmic upgrades.
- 18 trophies, auto-save, and offline earnings (up to 2 hours).

# ⛏️ Deepwild

An original 2D sandbox survival game: dig down, build up, survive the night.

Open `deepwild/index.html` in any browser. No install, no build. Works with keyboard and mouse, and on touch screens with on-screen buttons.

- **Generated world** every time: meadows, frozen pine forests and dunes on the surface, then caves and caverns through dirt, stone, slate and the glowing Cinder Depths full of magma.
- **Mine and build** with 30+ tile types: chop trees, dig tunnels, place blocks, platforms, glass and torches. Light really matters underground.
- **Craft** at a Workbench, Furnace and Anvil: smelt Copper, Iron, Glimmer and Ember ore into bars for better pickaxes, swords and mail. Better pickaxes reach deeper layers.
- **Day and night**: Gloops hop around by day, Hollows come out at night, and bats, Rock Crawlers and Ember Wisps live underground.
- **Boss**: craft a Dusk Idol and use it at night to call the Gloomwarden.
- Health upgrades (Vigor Roots), healing tonics, treasure pots, auto-save.

Controls: **A/D** move · **Space/W** jump · **S** drop through platforms · **Left mouse** use the selected item (mine, swing, place, eat) · **1–0 / wheel** hotbar · **E** bag & crafting · **M** sound · **H** hints · **Esc** menu.
