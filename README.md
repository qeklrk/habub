# 💪 Swole to the Stars

An idle/clicker game: lift weights in your mom's garage until you become **intergalactic**.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no build.

- **Lift** (click, tap, or hold Space) to earn strength and money. Fast lifting builds a combo multiplier.
- **Shop**: 20 upgrades, from heavier plates and protein shakes to clone labs, alien coaches and Dyson Sphere gyms.
- **Travel** through 11 places: Garage → Gym → Pro Competition → Everest → Orbit → Moon → Mars → Jupiter → the Sun → Andromeda (INTERGALACTIC) → Edge of the Universe.
- **Meteors** ⭐ fly by now and then. Click them for cash, strength, or a 7x frenzy.
- **Big Bang rebirth**: once intergalactic, reset for permanent Stardust boosts and cosmic upgrades.
- 18 trophies, auto-save, and offline earnings (up to 2 hours).
