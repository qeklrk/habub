Adds the Alchemist, a town NPC who sells potions.

- Moves in after you defeat the Eater of Worlds or the Brain of Cthulhu.
- Pre-Hardmode: Healing, Regeneration and Wormhole potions.
- After the Wall of Flesh: 3 more potions.
- After Plantera: combat potions.
- After the Moon Lord: every potion.

Calamity Mod is optional. When it is loaded, the Alchemist also sells Calamity potions as you progress.
